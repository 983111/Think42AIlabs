Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Think 42 AI Labs handles information when you visit think42ailabs.me, contact the team, or use forms and links provided on this website.

1. About Think 42 AI Labs

Think 42 AI Labs is an independent, student-led initiative that builds and shares open-source software, educational resources, experiments, and research-oriented projects. The website presents the initiative and links to projects and services that may be hosted separately.

Think 42 AI Labs is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), IFM, or the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI). Names are used only where necessary to describe independent projects, technologies, educational topics, or competitions. No institutional relationship or endorsement is implied.

2. Information you provide

You can browse the public website without creating an account. If you contact us by email, submit a community or participation form, or otherwise communicate with the team, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name or preferred name.
  • Your email address and the content of your message.
  • Information you include in a participation, collaboration, feedback, or project enquiry.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through general contact channels.

3. Technical information

When the website is requested, the website host and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Depending on the provider, this can include IP address, browser and device information, requested page, timestamps, and error or security logs. These records are generally controlled by the relevant hosting or infrastructure provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

The website also loads the Outfit typeface through Google Fonts. A request for that font may be sent to Google and handled under Google's privacy terms. Links to external websites and services may similarly disclose information to those services when you choose to visit them.

4. How information is used

Information provided directly to us may be used to:

  • Respond to questions, feedback, and collaboration enquiries.
  • Review community or project participation requests.
  • Communicate about a request or activity you have asked to join.
  • Maintain the security and operation of our public projects and website.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and address misuse or security incidents.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use contact details submitted to us for unrelated advertising campaigns.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not make personal information publicly available or sell it. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the website or handle a request, such as website hosting, email, form, or infrastructure providers. They process information under their own service terms and applicable arrangements.

If you use a third-party form, platform, repository, model service, or project application linked from this website, your interaction with that service is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not control the data practices of independent third parties.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The public website is designed to provide its core pages without requiring an account. The website code may use ordinary browser functionality to navigate between sections. The hosting provider or third-party resources may use technical mechanisms, including cookies or logs, according to their own operation. We do not promise that third-party destinations linked from this website are cookie-free.

7. Retention and security

We keep messages and enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant project records, resolve follow-up matters, or meet legal and security requirements. Technical logs are retained according to the relevant provider's settings and policies. Retention periods can vary by service.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to a volunteer-led, open-source initiative to limit access to information we receive. No website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information that you have sent directly to us, subject to information we need to retain for legitimate operational, legal, or security reasons. You may also choose not to provide optional information, although this may prevent us from responding to a request or considering a participation enquiry.

To make a privacy request, email team@think42ailabs.me. Please describe the information and request clearly. Do not include sensitive credentials in your message.

9. Children and educational resources

Our website may be useful to students, including younger learners. The public website is not designed to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to us may contact us at the address above and request appropriate action.

10. External links and open-source projects

Our website may link to source-code repositories, documentation, research publications, learning platforms, app stores, and other websites. Visiting or using those destinations is optional. Their operators determine their own data collection, account requirements, cookies, and retention practices. Review their policies before submitting information.

Making a project open source does not mean that personal information in an issue, discussion, contribution, or public profile is private. Consider carefully what you publish in public repositories and community spaces.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the website, projects, or the way we handle information changes. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the website after a revision means you have had an opportunity to review the updated policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Think 42 AI Labs at team@think42ailabs.me.
