K2 Horizon Lab ↗

An experimentation and learning initiative for exploring modern AI models, research workflows, and practical applications.

  • Model playground: test and compare AI models.
  • Fine-tuning: adapt models to specific tasks and domains.
  • Notebooks and simulations: guided experiments that explain system behaviour.
  • Benchmarks: track experiments against defined evaluation criteria.

IOAI Lab ↗

A learning and preparation initiative that helps high-school students get ready for the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence.

  • Guided learning: AI and ML concepts in a structured progression.
  • Coding practice: exercises that build practical programming skills.
  • Olympiad preparation: the reasoning and problem-solving competitions demand.
  • Task index: organized practice tasks for independent study.

About

Open access to serious AI education

Our mission is to give students a real platform to contribute to research and engineering that matters beyond the classroom. Our vision is to become a recognized student hub for AI work, producing publications, useful open-source tools, and members equipped to lead.

Founded and led by Vishwajeet Adkine.

Organisational status and independence. Think 42 AI Labs is currently an independent, student-led initiative and community project. It is not yet incorporated or registered as a company. It is not affiliated with, operated by, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Government of Abu Dhabi, any Abu Dhabi government entity, MBZUAI, or G42. References to MBZUAI and G42 on this website are for inspiration and context only; they do not indicate a partnership, institutional relationship, or endorsement.

Our vision

K2 Horizon Lab represents the lab's commitment to advancing practical AI experimentation and research, while IOAI Lab represents its commitment to making advanced AI education accessible to younger learners.

Together, they establish two complementary directions: building AI systems and developing the people who will build them.
