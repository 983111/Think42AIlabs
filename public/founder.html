Founder profile

Vishwajeet Shashikant Adkine

Founder & Lead, Think 42 AI Labs

Vishwajeet Shashikant Adkine is the Founder and Lead of Think 42 AI Labs, an independent, student-led initiative focused on artificial intelligence, machine learning, research and applied engineering. His work brings together technical experimentation, software development and projects designed around practical problems.

About

Through Think 42 AI Labs, he helps coordinate a community working across AI research, machine learning and software engineering. The initiative supports collaborative learning, technical experimentation and the development of practical AI systems.

His project work includes CeryS AI, an agricultural assistance application; K2 Horizon Lab, an environment for model experimentation and fine-tuning; and IOAI Lab, a learning initiative for students exploring artificial intelligence and Olympiad-style problem solving.

He documents technical work and project development through his portfolio, writing and public code repositories.
